## Quality standard

The work prioritizes useful visitor resources and accurate business citations. Google identifies low-quality directory links, automated link schemes and thin location doorway pages as spam patterns in its [Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam). Adding markup or receiving an application confirmation does not prove indexing, ranking gains or a new backlink.
